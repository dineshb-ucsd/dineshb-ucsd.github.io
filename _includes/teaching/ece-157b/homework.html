Homework is released on Tuesdays and due two weeks later on Saturday at 11:59 PM. Submissions go through Gradescope, and collaboration is encouraged as long as discussions and outside resources are cited clearly.

Assignments

Homework and lab sequence

HW 1 / Lab 1

Signal processing basics and MATLAB with simulated breathing traces and time-domain recovery.

HW 2 / Lab 2

Build an FMCW model and introduce range FFT ideas for breathing-rate recovery.

HW 3 / Lab 3

Work from real data and construct a breathing pipeline from measured wireless traces.

HW 4 / Lab 4

Estimate location and breathing jointly using angle-of-arrival and multi-antenna reasoning.

Final assignment

Recover breathing and heart-rate for multiple users in a more realistic sensing setting.
